# Governance and editorial decisions

**Current state (2 October 2026):** Federico Pacheco is the project owner. No external editor, reviewer, maintainer, coauthor or reproducer is represented here as appointed. The site has a private submission queue and owner-key editor; individual editorial accounts and delegated publication are planned work, not an active capability.

## Roles

Anyone may propose a public correction, source, translation, evaluation or reproduction. An editor screens eligibility, provenance, duplicates, interests, privacy and wording. A reviewer checks the source and proposed decision. A publisher applies an approved change and records its version. The owner remains accountable for rights, security, releases and appointments. People take a named role only after they accept it and access is provisioned individually. A contributor's proposal does not confer paper authorship.

Until individual roles and an independent reviewer are operating, the owner may make routine editorial decisions but must identify them as internal. A source checked by two AI systems is not a human or independent review. An interested editor must disclose the interest and seek a different reviewer where one is available; otherwise the limit is recorded with the decision. No one reviews their own evaluation as independent validation.

## Decision path

The public catalog's operational state is D1. GitHub issues, pull requests and the site form are proposal channels, not competing published catalogs. The editor records the source, proposed change and decision; the publisher creates a revision and a separately versioned public release when authorized. Frozen releases are immutable. A rejection receives a reason, and a correction or appeal can request a second source reading without erasing the previous decision. Security, privacy, rights, policy and release decisions stay with the owner until a written delegation is accepted.

The current application does not yet store every required decision field or implement separate identities. The [implementation plan](docs/COMMUNITY-IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) tracks that gap; this document does not imply the workflow is complete.

## Credit and authorship

Public credit is opt-in, at the contributor's chosen name or pseudonym. A record correction, source review, translation, software change, evaluation and reproduction receive distinct credit. Paper authorship requires substantive intellectual contribution, approval of the manuscript and accountability for the work, agreed by the actual coauthors under the destination venue's rules. Suggestions, funding, software operation or being named as a prospective collaborator do not automatically confer authorship. A DOI identifies a deposited version; it does not mean peer review.

## Amendments and accountability

Propose policy changes through a public issue unless they contain private material. The owner records the rationale and effective date. A reviewer appointment, authority to publish, retention setting and private reporting channel require an operational change and an accepted responsibility, not merely a text edit. Editorial and moderation appeals go to the owner through a private channel agreed with the affected person; no unpublished case details are posted publicly.
