# Code of conduct

Discuss sources, methods and limitations respectfully. Evidence-based disagreement is welcome. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention and pressure to publish an unsupported claim are unacceptable in project spaces.

The project owner may ask for edits, hide or remove material, limit participation or block an account. Responses should be proportionate and recorded privately where they concern a person. Affected participants may appeal a decision to the owner; if the owner is involved in the dispute, an independent moderator must be appointed before claiming an independent appeal was available. Do not post private evidence in a public issue to make a conduct report. At this stage the project has not designated an external moderator or published a verified private conduct inbox; contact the owner through an agreed private channel until one is established.
