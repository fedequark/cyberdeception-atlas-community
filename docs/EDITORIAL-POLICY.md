# Editorial policy

## Purpose and scope

Cyberdeception Atlas is an open, source-linked reference for practitioners and researchers. A published record is a description of a reviewed source, not an endorsement or an independent test. Coverage is a curated selection, never a claim of worldwide completeness.

## Inclusion

Include a resource when its primary source is public, the work is relevant to defensive cyber deception or a clearly labeled adjacent field, and the source permits a bounded description. Record its type, technique, environment, provenance, review date and limitations. Prefer stable original repository, publisher, institutional or provider pages. Archive a record when its primary source can no longer support the description.

## Evidence labels

`source-page`, `repository-metadata`, `publisher-metadata`, `abstract` and `full-text` describe what the editor actually read. They are not quality scores. A full-text label requires reading the complete publication and recording its method, evidence, results and limitations. Vendor case studies and product claims remain attributed to their publishers. No record claims comparative effectiveness without a comparable evaluation.

## Interests and original work

They use the same inclusion and evidence rules as every other record and cannot be labeled independent evaluations of his work. Any additional owner, vendor or reviewer interest must be disclosed on the affected record before publication.

## Geography and language

Organization location, documented delivery regions, field-report location, sector and source language are separate concepts. Enter a value only if a cited primary source supports that exact field. An unknown value is retained as unknown; it is not interpreted as nonavailability.

Public coverage reporting limits organization country to products and services, where the concept is meaningful and supported by a primary source. Deployment regions are not offered as a library filter or public export field because primary sources rarely state them precisely. Sector is not included in the public export; it may be reintroduced for case studies only when the publisher explicitly names the sector. These limits prevent sparse fields from implying coverage the Atlas cannot substantiate.

Search quality is measured with daily counts of searches that returned no records, split by interface language. Search terms and visitor identifiers are not stored in that count.

## Corrections and versioning

Suggestions enter a private review queue. The editor checks the primary source, duplicate status and claim scope before publishing. A material correction records a revision, source and review date. Automated monitoring creates review reminders only. Public catalog snapshots and private D1 backups support recovery without publishing private submissions or audit entries.

## Maintenance cadence

Triage public suggestions weekly, review source-change reminders monthly, and update field syntheses quarterly. Run a bounded full-catalog link audit separately from the daily incremental monitor. HTTP 403, 406, timeouts and bot blocks require manual review; only a confirmed 404/410 is a broken source.
